About

Right now I lead AI adoption for Corporate IT at Deutsche Börse Group in Luxembourg. That means finding the use cases worth building with the people who do the work, onboarding teams to DAISY, the group's GenAI platform, and designing our first voice AI agent.

I got here through security. I joined the group as an information security engineer, moved on to building agents on DAISY, and kept the habits: threat models first, one gateway for model traffic, least privilege for tools. Before that I tested vulnerability checks at Tenable in Paris.

I'm also finishing an M.Sc. in Data Science at Universität Trier, with a thesis on fine-tuning language models to find bugs in code. Away from the desk I swim (I competed at state level), play football and practise my French.
